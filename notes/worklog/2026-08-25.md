  - 读上游 `NitroGateway.Domain.Measurements/BatchMeasurements.cs`、`MeasurementRecord.cs`、`Devices/DataType.cs`、`QualityCode.cs`、`Forwarder/JsonMessageSerializer.cs`、`Forwarder.cs` 确认：topic 三段式；System.Text.Json camelCase；`dataType`/`quality` 序列化为**整数枚举码**（Bool=0…String=10，Good=0）；时间戳 `yyyy-MM-ddTHH:mm:ss.fffffffZ`；每轮 batch/record `id` 均为新 GUID；`successCount`=quality==Good 的记录数。
